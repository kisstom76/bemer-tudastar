# BEMER in the UK and Ireland – official set-up and Irish rules for an independent partner (as of 2026-10-13)

Research scope: (A) how BEMER officially operates in the UK/Ireland; (B) an overview of the rules for an independent BEMER partner living in Dublin. The web research ran on 2026-10-13. Sources were limited: BEMER's own country pages give little information, and several pages (imprint, financing, en-gb shop) returned 404 to the fetch tool.

## A1. BEMER UK entity, office, customer service, webshop, UK Responsible Person, prices

### Takeaway
I found no active BEMER-owned UK company. The only BEMER-named UK company was dissolved in 2018. UK and Irish customers are served centrally by BEMER Int. AG (Liechtenstein) through a shared customer-service email and a Liechtenstein (+423) phone number. The en_GB section of bemergroup.com exists but shows no GBP prices or shop. The UK Responsible Person could not be identified.

No UK or IE address or local entity is named. The page identifies the company as "BEMER Int. AG is a medical technology company" — [BEMER Contact us (en_GB)](https://bemergroup.com/en_GB/Support/Contact-us/)
- The en_GB home page lists the Human Line products (Premium-Set Evo, Light-Pack-Evo, Evo Controller, Evo Applicators, Evo Accessories). It shows **no prices and no shop**. There is a "Financing" link in the header and footer, but its content could not be retrieved (404). Regulatory wording on the page: "Certified medical product", "Class IIa in Europe" — [bemergroup.com/en_GB/home](http://bemergroup.com/en_GB/home/)

- General UK rule: a non-UK manufacturer must appoint a UK Responsible Person and register its devices with the MHRA before placing them on the GB market, whether they carry CE or UKCA marking. CE marking remains accepted in GB for a transitional period (reported as until 2028–2030 depending on type). For a CE-marked device the UKRP need not appear on the label — [Casus Consulting](https://casusconsulting.com/services/uk/device-registration/); [Labelservice](https://labelservice.co.uk/mhra-tightens-ukca-marking-rules-for-medical-devices-as-ce-transition-deadlines-near/)

## A2. Rental, trial and finance programmes in the UK; UK partner websites and claim wording

### Takeaway
I found no official BEMER UK rental, trial or consumer-finance programme. Paid trials and rentals in the UK are run by individual partners (e.g. a 2-month rental for £899). Partner-site health claims range from hedged ("support circulation") to clearly medicinal (pain, recovery, "+30% oxygen delivery"). Only some sites carry disclaimers.

### Cited Findings
- **Elevate You (Aylesbury, Iwona Sood)** describes itself as an authorised BEMER distributor. It offers a "strict 2-month rental program (£899)" shipped UK-wide with video-call onboarding, and says the rental fee "may be considered toward a purchase". No purchase price is shown and no Klarna or other finance is mentioned. Claim wording includes "enhance oxygen delivery by up to 30%", "chronic pain", "faster athletic recovery" and "evidence-based health solutions" (no studies cited). Disclaimer-type statements: BEMER is "considered a private, complementary treatment in the UK", "not routinely funded by the NHS", a "Class IIa Medical Device" under MDR. It also warns against buying second-hand units on eBay or Facebook Marketplace — [elevateyou.uk/bemer-uk](https://elevateyou.uk/bemer-uk/)
- **Body & Mind Shop (Barnstaple, Devon, EX31 2JT)** calls itself "a UK John Woolf BEMER partner" and offers "secure UK ordering support" for the Premium-Set Evo, with no device price shown. It sells "Osteomyology and BEMER Therapy Session: £40.00 – £240.00". Claim wording: "Support healthy circulation, oxygen delivery, and recovery after physical activity". Disclaimers: "not been evaluated by the MHRA or FDA" and "not intended to diagnose, cure or prevent any disease". **The homepage did not show an "8-week home trial £800" offer**, and searches did not confirm one — [bodyandmindshop.com](https://www.bodyandmindshop.com/)
- **The Horse's Back** sends buyers to the international BEMER store. Its claims include "Reintroduce optimal wellness to your life!" and "By improving restricted blood flow, BEMER can help the body to perform its functions optimally". No medical disclaimer was found — [shop.thehorsesback.com](https://shop.thehorsesback.com/products/bemer-for-people-uk)
- An 8-week trial format exists elsewhere in the BEMER network. A Norwegian ad describes an 8-week trial where the trial fee is deducted from the purchase price if the buyer keeps the device. No GBP price was found — [finn.no](https://www.finn.no/346979714)
- UK CAP Code rule 12.1: medicinal claims may be made only for MHRA-licensed medicines or for medical devices with the applicable conformity marking. In late 2025 the ASA upheld a series of rulings against wellness and medical claims, including influencer content — [CMS](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/wellness-brands-under-scrutiny-asa-draws-a-hard-line-on-mhra-regulated-claims); [Global Legal Post](https://www.globallegalpost.com/news/selling-wellness-in-the-uk-under-the-advertising-standards-authoritys-rules-227434993)

### Inferences
- In the UK, rental and trial are partner-level business models. Partners own the device and hire it out, then sell it, rather than BEMER running a programme. For a Hungarian partner in London this means a rental would be the partner's own contract and own risk.
- The "8-week home trial £800" example could not be verified as of 2026-10-13. It may sit on a sub-page that was not fetched, or it may have been removed.

### Gaps
- Whether BEMER offers a finance partner for UK consumers (Klarna, PayPal, Novuna or similar): unknown. The en_GB "Financing" page returned 404.
- Whether BEMER formally authorises or restricts partner rental schemes: no public source found.

## B. Ireland (Dublin) – overview for an independent partner

### Takeaway
Ireland applies EU MDR directly, and HPRA is the regulator. An Ireland-based distributor of medical devices registers with HPRA. Consumer hire over 3 months is regulated (Central Bank authorisation), and CCD2 applies from 20 November 2026. Advertising follows ASAI Code section 11, which requires robust human-trial evidence. There is no national beauty or therapy licence. VAT thresholds are €42,500 for services and €85,000 for goods.

### Cited Findings
**B1 – Medical devices (HPRA, MDR)**
- HPRA defines a distributor as an EU entity, other than the manufacturer or importer, that makes a device available on the market. Distributors based in Ireland must register their organisation details and device types with HPRA through its online form and receive an HPRA registration number. The legal basis is MDR Chapter III and S.I. 691/2021 — [HPRA distributors](https://www.hpra.ie/regulation/medical-devices/importers-and-distributors/distributors); [HPRA registration](https://www.hpra.ie/regulation/medical-devices/registration/distributors)
- A fee of about €145 is reported by a third-party source, not confirmed by HPRA. EUDAMED module timing (reported mandatory from 28 May 2026) is also third-party only — [RegDesk](https://www.regdesk.co/blog/ireland-reg/)

**B2 – Consumer hire / credit**
- Consumer Credit Act 1995: a "consumer-hire agreement" is a hire lasting more than 3 months, where the goods remain the owner's property — [Irish Legal Guide](https://legalguide.ie/consumer-credit-2/2/); [CCA 1995 revised](https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/1995/act/24/revised/en/html)
- The Consumer Protection (Regulation of Retail Credit and Credit Servicing Firms) Act 2022 brought consumer-hire and hire-purchase providers under Central Bank of Ireland authorisation and the Consumer Protection Code — [Grant Thornton](https://www.grantthornton.ie/insights/factsheets/consumer-protection-regulation-of-retail-credit-and-credit-servicing-firms-act-2022/)
- CCD2 (Directive 2023/2225): transposition was due by 20 November 2025, with the rules applying from **20 November 2026**. The Department of Finance plans to transpose by ministerial regulations — [Dept. of Finance outcome statement](https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/03edb4d2/CCD2_Public_Consultation_Outcome_Statement.pdf). A September 2026 secondary source says the Irish regulations were not yet published — [web60.ie](https://web60.ie/blog/pay-later-checkout-rules-ireland) (secondary, unconfirmed)

**B3 – Advertising**
- ASAI Code section 11 covers medicines, medical devices, treatments, health and beauty products. Section 11.1 requires substantiation that, where relevant, includes "robust and reputable trials on human subjects". The ASAI offers free, non-binding copy advice — [ASAI ruling Health & Beauty](https://adstandards.ie/complaint/health-beauty-91/); [Fieldfisher](https://www.fieldfisher.com/en-ie/locations/ireland/ireland-blog/code-standards-advertising-marketing-communications-ireland)
- CCPC (Competition and Consumer Protection Commission) is the statutory consumer-protection enforcer. Not researched in depth.

**B4 – Treatment licensing, insurance**
- No authoritative Irish government source was found. Indicative sources say no national licence is needed to open a salon, though these are hairdressing-focused, and Ireland is described as lightly regulated for beauty treatments. Planning and health-and-safety rules still apply — [Loghouse](https://www.loghouse.ie/how-to-set-up-a-home-salon-in-a-garden-room/); [IPSC](https://ipsc.ptools.net/browse-by-sector/other-services/hairdresser)
- Insurance: public, product and treatment liability cover is recommended. Associated Irish Therapists (AIT) is named as an Irish insurance and membership provider in an older forum (2009) — [SalonGeek](https://www.salongeek.com/threads/insurance-anyone-with-information.59581/) (old, informal)

**B5 – Tax**
- VAT registration thresholds: **€85,000 for goods and €42,500 for services**, raised from €80,000 and €40,000 by Finance Act 2024 with effect from 1 January 2025. They are unchanged in Budget 2026. There is no threshold for businesses not established in Ireland — [Xero IE](https://www.xero.com/ie/guides/vat-threshold-ireland/); [Kintsugi](https://trykintsugi.com/sales-tax-guides/europe/ireland). A Numeral guide still cites €37,500 and €75,000, which are outdated figures — [Numeral](https://www.numeral.com/blog/ireland-vat-rates-and-compliance)
- Ireland–Hungary double tax convention: signed 25 April 1995 and given effect by S.I. 301/1995, modified by the MLI (effective from 1 January 2022) — [Revenue treaty PDF](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/documents/double-taxation-treaties/h/hungary.pdf); [MLI synthesised text](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/documents/double-taxation-treaties/h/synthesised-text-mli-ireland-hungary-dtc.pdf)
- Sole-trader registration with Revenue (income tax, Form TR1, via ROS/myAccount): not separately researched. This is standard Revenue procedure.

**B6 – VAT relief for disabled persons' aids**
- VAT (Refund of Tax) (No. 15) Order 1981 / Form VAT61A: a refund of VAT on goods "specially constructed or adapted" for a disabled person, or reasonably treated as such. Claims are decided case by case, with medical evidence that the item is essential. The refund is **not available for services or for rental of goods** — [Revenue VAT61A](https://Www.revenue.ie/en/vat/documents/form-vat61a.pdf); [Oireachtas PQ 2018](https://www.oireachtas.ie/en/debates/question/2018-04-17/211/); [Oireachtas PQ 2024](https://www.oireachtas.ie/en/debates/question/2024-01-31/25/)
- Some medical appliances (orthopaedic appliances, walking frames and similar) are zero-rated — [Oireachtas PQ 2018](https://www.oireachtas.ie/en/debates/question/2018-04-17/211/)

### Inferences
- A Dublin partner who buys, holds or resells devices, or rents them out, likely acts as an MDR "distributor" and should check HPRA registration. If the partner only refers customers to the BEMER webshop and BEMER Int. AG ships directly, registration may not be needed. This is an interpretation, not confirmed by HPRA.
- A partner's own hire to consumers running more than 3 months probably falls under consumer-hire rules requiring Central Bank authorisation. Shorter rentals (e.g. 8 weeks) are probably outside the 1995 Act's consumer-hire definition, but CCD2 transposition may change scope from 20 November 2026. This needs legal confirmation.
- A BEMER device is general-purpose rather than "specially constructed or adapted" for a disability. The VAT61A refund is therefore unlikely to apply, and it is excluded for rentals in any case. Not confirmed by a Revenue ruling.
- BEMER commission income for a Dublin resident is likely taxable in Ireland as self-employment income, with the Ireland–Hungary treaty preventing double taxation. This depends on actual residence and needs advice.

### Gaps
- No BEMER Ireland-specific page, partner or rental offer was found in Dublin.
- Final Irish CCD2 regulations and Central Bank guidance: not found.
- An authoritative source on PEMF treatment licensing in Ireland: not found.
- Current HPRA fee and EUDAMED status: only third-party sources.
